Our Story

Rooted in Fresno, grown with love.

Flower City Fresno started as a small neighborhood flower stand with one simple goal: bring fresh, beautiful, affordable flowers to every corner of the Central Valley. Today we’re proud to be a go-to local florist for birthdays, anniversaries, weddings, and everyday “just because” moments.

Every stem is hand-selected and every arrangement is designed in-house by our team of local florists—never a cookie-cutter order from a national wire service.
