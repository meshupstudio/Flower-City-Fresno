About Us

Welcome to Flower City Restaurant

Where culinary excellence meets the vibrant world of Chinese food. Located in Fresno, CA, this gem offers a delightful blend of traditional flavors and modern convenience, ensuring a memorable dining experience. Whether you’re ordering takeout or delivery, every dish reflects freshness and quality.

Must-Try Dishes: Don’t miss our exceptional wonton soup and spicy orange chicken, both rave-reviewed by patrons for their robust flavors.

Vegetarian Options: A diverse selection caters to all, including plentiful vegetarian choices.

Experience the joy of comfort food at Flower City; it’s more than just a meal—it’s a delightful escape!
